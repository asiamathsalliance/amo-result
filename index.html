Asia Mathematics Olympics

The Asia Mathematics Olympiad (AMO) is an international competition that aims to allow students to reveal and embrace their talent in Mathematics. More than 180,000 students from countries such as Japan, China, Hong Kong and Korea have participated in this competition since 1991.

About Us

Asia Math Alliance

We support students and schools through high-quality mathematics competitions and enrichment pathways. Our mission is to build confidence, curiosity, and problem-solving excellence by connecting learners across Southeast Asia with meaningful contest experiences.
